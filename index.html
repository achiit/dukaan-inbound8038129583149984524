    Same shop, same promise, since 1990

    I opened Aroodh Jyoti in 1990 with one idea: a family should never have to wonder if the dal is good or the weight is right. Many of today’s customers first came here with their parents.

    We sit near the Shiva statue on the Mudhol–Bollayi road, and our doors open at 6 in the morning, because farmers and workers start early. Come in, ask the price, check the quality yourself.

    • 100% quality products, checked before they reach the shelf

    • Order on WhatsApp, collect without waiting
